Ch 2 · Particles & models

e/m = 1.7588×1011 C/kg (same for all gases ⇒ electron universal) · e = 1.602×10−19 C (Millikan ⇒ charge quantised) · p 1837× e− · n slightly > p.

Anode rays: e/m varies — they are the ionised gas atoms; mass and charge both change.

α-scattering: most through → empty space · few deflect → charge concentrated · 1 in 20000 rebounds → also massive. Nucleus 10−15, atom 10−10 m.

Rutherford drawbacks (2): accelerating e− radiates → spirals in ~10−8 s; would give continuous not line spectrum.

Ch 2 · Radiation, photon, photoelectric

$c=\nu\lambda$  ·  $\bar\nu=1/\lambda$  ·  $E=h\nu=\frac{hc}{\lambda}=nh\nu$

$h\nu=h\nu_0+\tfrac12 m_ev^2$  ·  $\phi=h\nu_0$  ·  KE$_{max}=h(\nu-\nu_0)$

Graph KE vs ν: straight line, slope = h (same all metals ⇒ parallel), x-intercept = ν0. No emission below ν0.

4 observations: threshold ν0 · instantaneous · KE ∝ frequency · number ∝ intensity.

Ch 3 · The four anomalies

Be > B, Mg > Al (IE): B's e− from 2p — higher energy, better shielded — vs Be's filled 2s2.

N > O, P > S (IE): N is half-filled 2p3, symmetric & stable; O's paired 2p e− repels ⇒ easier to remove.

Cl > F, S > O (ΔegH more −ve): F/O so small that compact 2p causes inter-electronic repulsion.

2nd ΔegH always +ve; noble gases +ve.

Ch 4 · Polarity & H-bonding

μ = 0: BeF2, CO2, BF3, CCl4 (symmetric). H2O 1.85, NH3 1.47 > NF3 0.24.

NH3 vs NF3: in NH3 bond dipoles point toward N, adding to the lone-pair dipole; in NF3 they point away, opposing it.

H-bond needs H on F, O or N. 10–40 kJ/mol. Inter → raises bp; intra (o-nitrophenol) → lowers bp. Ice open cage ⇒ less dense.

Resonance: only e− move, nuclei fixed; forms not real; hybrid lower in energy; O3 both bonds 128 pm.

Ch 7 · Balancing (half-reaction)
  1. Split into oxidation & reduction halves
  2. Balance atoms except H, O
  3. Balance O with H2O
  4. Balance H with H+
  5. Balance charge with e−
  6. Multiply so e− cancel; add
  7. Basic: add OH− to both sides = no. of H+; combine to H2O

Final look — the traps
  1. STP 22.7 L at 1 bar, 22.4 at 1 atm.
  2. Molarity T-dependent; molality not.
  3. Limiting reagent: moles ÷ coefficient.
  4. Photoelectric KE → frequency; current → intensity.
  5. Bohr: one-electron species only.
  6. Series named by landing level.
  7. Total nodes = n−1. s orbital L = 0.
  8. l never = n (no 2d, 1p, 3f).
  9. 4s fills first, empties first.
  10. Isoelectronic: more Z ⇒ smaller.
  11. Geometry ≠ shape (H2O bent; XeF4 sq planar).
  12. O2 paramagnetic — MOT only.
  13. Adding e− to O2 weakens it (antibonding).
  14. Basic medium → finish with OH−.
  15. E° values are never multiplied by coefficients.
